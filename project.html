Residential Garden
— The Gap

A small garden project shaped by the existing space, the client's preferences and the process of learning by doing.

A little about this project

This project involved refreshing an established residential garden. I spent time looking at the existing plants, the condition of the soil and how the space could feel more balanced without losing its natural character.

My part in it

I worked on preparing the soil, considering plant placement and bringing together different heights and textures. I also paid attention to how each plant related to the surrounding space as the layout developed.

Looking back

What I enjoyed most was taking the time to understand the site before making changes. I am still learning how to make planting decisions with more confidence, and each project gives me something new to notice for next time.
