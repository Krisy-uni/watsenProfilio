About Watsen

I enjoy working outdoors, learning about plants and finding thoughtful ways to shape garden spaces. This website is a place to keep a record of the work I do, the things I notice and what I learn along the way.

Work · Plants · Design

Learning through doing

I believe good work comes from paying attention: to a place, to the people who use it and to the plants that grow there. I am interested in the relationship between practical gardening, thoughtful design and the natural character of a space.

This archive will change as I do. It is not a finished statement, but an honest record of practice, curiosity and gradual progress.
